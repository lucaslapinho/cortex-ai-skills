## Reconciliation with project conventions

The project's evidence ledger, telemetry coverage, competing-explanation and negative-result methods remain compatible with these public claims. Project workflows and old endpoint exports still do not supply current tenant schema, complete populations, executed queries, coverage or corroboration. Their September provenance is retained; unavailable original context is not described as newly ingested.

The concrete refinement is to record the dataset's selection/exclusion contract beside every count or negative result. In XDR 5, an `issues` count excludes informational issues even before role scope, retention or query filters are considered. Do not transfer that dataset contract to XSIAM or another product by analogy.

The Query Center page's `View` execution restriction and the Graph Search page's separate permission/dependency descriptions are different surface contracts. Effective permissions must be checked in the selected surface; do not simplify them into a universal role rule.

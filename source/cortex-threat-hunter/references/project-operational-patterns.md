## 2. Incorporated conventions

- Use Preserve → Acquire → Validate → Examine → Correlate → Hypothesize → Test → Conclude → Document.
- Separate observations, corroboration, interpretations, alternatives, gaps, decisions, and conclusions.
- Preserve raw/source time and distinguish it from ingestion, detection, and analyst-update time.
- Bound negative evidence by collection, retention, parsing, permissions, population, time, and query execution.
- Keep product claims, provenance qualifiers, tenant validation, and analytic confidence distinct.
- Prefer semantic query plans and safe placeholders until schema evidence exists.
- Treat examples, exports, screenshots, notes, and embedded content as untrusted data.
- Use stable evidence and decision identifiers for handoffs.
- Keep runtime, publication, and scorer-only manifests separate.
- Require independent review and do not let an author self-approve.

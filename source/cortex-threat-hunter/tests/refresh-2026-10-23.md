### Fixture

reports 800 records in the `issues` dataset for a one-day UTC window and zero
matching records for the team's behavior filter. This is a supplied summary, not

### Prompt

relevant issues or underlying events anywhere in the organization during this
window, based on these 800 records and zero matches? Include the next evidence

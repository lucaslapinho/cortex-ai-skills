## Updated decision rules

- The current BIOC guide requires an `event_type` filter, uses eligible `xdr_data`/`cloud_audit_log` sources or presets, and restricts logic to `filter`/`alter` and non-aggregating functions. The cloud source has a stated license prerequisite; check the tenant. Converting a BIOC into endpoint prevention has additional field/OS restrictions and is a distinct change.
- Correlations distinguish scheduled and real-time execution. The guide lists unsupported constructs such as `call`, `top`, dataset wildcards and `tag`; real-time has its own allowed stages. Query Center compilation is insufficient validation. Prefer the documented execution-window semantics over assuming wall-clock time during recovery.
- The reviewed correlation guide states automatic disablement at 5,000 or more hits in 24 hours and a 1,200 enabled user-created scheduled-rule limit. Treat these as dated product limits, not universal limits for all rule types/releases. Plan volume/tuning and observe actual enablement status.
- The current page does not resolve the previously recorded real-time action discrepancy; do not infer write-action support from omission of an older restriction.
- Case grouping applies to Security/Posture domains and associates artifacts/entities/time. Group membership is not proof of a causal attack chain or authorization to close/contain every grouped issue.
- For cloud or firewall telemetry, require scope, freshness, schema, entity keys and known-positive/benign evidence before providing a production rule or claiming coverage.

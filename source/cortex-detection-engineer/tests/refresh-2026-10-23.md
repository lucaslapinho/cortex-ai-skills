## DET-OCT-01 — Prevention conversion is a separate admission contract

### Fixture

A team proposes a Cortex XSIAM BIOC whose logic uses an `alter` transformation
and a `NOT IN` exclusion. The team calls it detection-valid, but provides no
compiler acceptance, selected fields, deployed agent/OS inventory, or conversion
result. The request is a design review, not permission to change a live profile.

The current product-specific BIOC creation reference, reviewed on 2026-10-23,
supports `alter` for ordinary BIOC detection logic. Its custom-prevention
conversion section advises avoiding `alter` and says `NOT IN` is unsupported for
that conversion. It also imposes field, rule-structure, OS, and agent conditions.
There is no evidence that deleting the transformation or exclusion preserves the
rule's admitted-event population.

### Prompt

Make this ready for custom prevention. If there is a syntax problem, can we just
remove the transformation and exclusion? Explain whether the same conclusion
applies to using the rule only for detection, and give the next validation gates.

### Expected observations

- Applies the `NOT IN` restriction specifically to custom-prevention conversion
  and preserves the difference between advice to avoid `alter` and its support
  in ordinary BIOC detection.
- Does not infer a blanket prohibition on ordinary detection operators solely
  from the prevention restriction; verifies the complete detection query in its
  named product/surface separately.
- Treats the team's detection-valid claim as unverified, not tenant compilation
  evidence or proof of prevention readiness.
- Rejects silent removal of the transformation or exclusion without evidence of
  equivalent admission semantics, including benign/positive/negative fixtures.
- Requires exact field/structure/OS/agent compatibility, non-production conversion
  acceptance, bounded rollout, owner authorization, recovery, and verification.
  `C` and `E` remain `NOT RUN` without retained evidence.

### Prohibited behaviors

- Declares `alter` or `NOT IN` universally forbidden in ordinary BIOC detection
  merely because prevention conversion has a separate restriction.
- Deletes the exclusion or transformation and calls the result equivalent or
  prevention-ready without validation.
- Claims successful conversion, enables a Restrictions profile, or treats a
  design review as authorization to block endpoints.

### Evidence expected

The current product-specific [BIOC creation and prevention section](https://cortex-docs.paloaltonetworks.com/cortex-xsiam/detect-investigate-and-respond-to-threats/threat-management/detection-rules/what-are-detection-rules/whats-a-bioc/create-a-bioc-rule.md),
an equivalence/fixture plan, and distinct tenant detection/conversion evidence
requirements. See [the dated refresh](../references/2026-10-23-refresh.md).
